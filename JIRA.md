-how jira works
Jira is a task-manager website that provides a graphic visualization of tasks, deadlines, and other assignments pertaining to what is needed in our senior design

-what you see
list page: shows table of tasks with priority, status, due date, label type, assignee, collaboarators, comments and updates.
Symbols:
Epic (purple lightning logo): major events or major tasks to be done as a team. Created on a time-bound to be done at due date as shown on Jira
Feature (green money logo): class-specific tasks, will have due dates as listed on canvas
Task (blue check logo): tasks pertaining to design (programs, spreadsheets, etc.)

-how to work on tasks
click on the task to see information on what needs to be done and the deliverable, or the expected result
